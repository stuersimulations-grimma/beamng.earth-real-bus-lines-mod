# beamng.earth-real-bus-lines-mod
Real Bus Lines Mod for BeamNG.Earth

Real Bus Lines mod for BeamNG.drive.

I am currently developing a mod that allows you to drive real-world bus routes using the default in-game bus. The mechanic is simple: you drive to the first stop of a route and confirm the start of the trip. You then follow the route and earn money. In the current version, this money doesn't have a specific use yet, but I plan to add features that will make earning it meaningful. Right now, you are penalized for accidents—money is deducted from your balance—though this system will evolve in the upcoming beta versions. My plan is that, by the beta stage at the latest, accident damage can no longer be fixed instantly via the repair button in the BeamNG menu; instead, you will have to drive to a workshop to get the bus repaired. In that scenario, the money would be deducted at the workshop rather than at the moment of the accident. Additionally, a basic "passenger satisfaction" system is already implemented: you start a route with 100% satisfaction, which decreases depending on your driving style. The system tracks accidents, harsh braking, abrupt acceleration (though this is difficult to trigger with an automatic transmission), and cornering at excessive speeds. There are currently three standard routes included:
1. B-Line: Grimma Train Station – Volkshausplatz
2. 610: Grimma Train Station – Bad Lausick
3. 610: Bad Lausick – Grimma Train Station

More routes will be added to the base mod, and additional routes will also be made available as optional downloads. I am open to requests for new routes, so feel free to share your ideas!

Furthermore, you can create your own routes using the provided template. A guide is included that explains the process in detail. As soon as the mod enters its public alpha phase, I will make it available here on GitHub.
